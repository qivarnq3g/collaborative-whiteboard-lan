# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while focusing discussion on technical evidence.
- Welcome questions and help others learn.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory language, or unwanted attention
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the [`qivarnq3g` GitHub profile](https://github.com/qivarnq3g). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone publicly represents the project or its community.
